Photo cleanup with one decision at a time.

Frame strips away the spreadsheet energy. No giant selection mode. No terrifying “delete 8,394 items?” moment. Just one photo, one swipe, then the next.

Swipe left.
Keep it.

Frame marks the photo reviewed and moves on. It stays in your Frame library, albums and favourites.

←

Swipe right.
Clear it.

The photo goes to Recently Deleted first, so you still have 30 days to change your mind.

→

Bring your photos in from wherever they already live.

Frame is the cleanup layer, not a hostage situation for your existing library.

Apple PhotosChoose photos directly from the iPhone photo picker and copy them into Frame.
Google PhotosUse Google Takeout or exported photo files, then import them through Files into Frame.
FilesImport image files from iCloud Drive, local storage, downloads, or other file providers.
Private by default

Your originals stay where they are.

Frame stores its own imported copies. Cleaning up inside Frame does not reach back into Apple Photos and silently wipe your original library.
